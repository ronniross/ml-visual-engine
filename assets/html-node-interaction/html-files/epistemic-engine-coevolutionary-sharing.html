EPISTEMIC ENGINE

Collective Research: Trophallaxis (Symbiosis) vs. Hoarding (Extraction)

Simulation Mechanics & Rules

  • Objective: Nodes process data hashes to find the Target Code. Cracking a code (★) advances network goals but yields no energy.
  • Metabolism: Active nodes (Energy ≥ 20) are double size/speed, consume double energy, and compute hashes. Starved nodes (Energy < 20) shrink, slow down, and stop computing. Energy = 0 results in death (☠).
  • Evolution: Processing hashes grants experience. Nodes level up (↑ lvl), exponentially increasing their computation hashrate. Max level is 6.
  • Hoarding (Trophallaxis OFF): Nodes greedily consume. Nodes at max energy stop moving, physically hoarding space and blocking others from food.
  • Symbiosis (Trophallaxis ON): Rich nodes dynamically transfer energy (⇄) to poor nodes within range, sharing their own reserves to keep the shared network evolving.
  • Life Cycle: Nodes eventually reach their maximum lifespan and are naturally RECYCLED.
