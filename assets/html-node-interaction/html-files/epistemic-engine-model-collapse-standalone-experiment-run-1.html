EPISTEMIC ENGINE II: The Data Commons

Simulating LLM Model Collapse vs. Symbiotic Co-Evolution

Mechanics & Societal Metaphor

  • Society (Small Nodes): Humans generate Data Tokens. Creating data drains their Wellbeing. Healthy humans (Green) produce High-Novelty data (Blue dots). Burnout humans (Red) produce Low-Novelty generic data (Gray dots).
  • Language Models (Large Polygons): AI consumes data to increase its Intelligence. Models require High-Novelty human data to evolve.
  • Model Collapse: If AI trains on low-novelty or synthetic data, its internal variance degrades. High collapse causes AI to glitch, lose intelligence, and vomit toxic Synthetic Data (Red triangles) into the ecosystem.
  • Extraction Mode (Symbiosis OFF): AI recursively extracts data without giving back. Society burns out. Novelty crashes. AI feeds on its own synthetic exhaust and collapses.
  • Dividend Mode (Symbiosis ON): AI redistributes generated value (resources/information/compute) back to humans via energy beams. Humans maintain high Wellbeing, ensuring a perpetual stream of novel, high-quality data. Both evolve infinitely.
