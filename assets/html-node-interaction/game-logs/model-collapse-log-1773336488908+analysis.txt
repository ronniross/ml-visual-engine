# 🔄 Epistemic Engine II: Intervention Study Analysis
## Log: `model-collapse-log-1773336488908.txt`

This third log introduces a critical experimental variable: **dynamic protocol switching**. Rather than testing pure Extraction vs. pure Dividend, this run simulates *policy oscillation*—the real-world scenario where extractive and symbiotic practices alternate. The results reveal something profound about resilience, tipping points, and the asymmetry of damage vs. repair.

### 🔬 Key Findings

#### 1. **The 16-Second Cliff**
When symbiosis was disabled at `17:25:38`, society wellbeing dropped below 30% in just **16 seconds**. This isn't gradual decline—it's a *phase transition*. Once the reciprocal flow stops, the human layer's buffer depletes catastrophically fast. 

> 🌿 *Ecological parallel*: Removing mycorrhizal networks from a forest doesn't slowly weaken trees—it triggers rapid nutrient collapse.

#### 2. **Asymmetric Collapse: Not All Models Fail Equally**
During the recovery phase (D), observe the divergence:
- **Model [1]**: Avoided severe collapse → continued evolving → reached **Tier 900**
- **Models [2] & [3]**: Crossed >60% collapse threshold → emitted synthetic ☠ data → degraded to **Tier 000/100**

This suggests collapse isn't purely deterministic. Stochastic factors—timing of data consumption, which humans were exhausted, momentary novelty fluctuations—create *path dependency*. Once a model starts regurgitating synthetic exhaust, it contaminates the commons for others.

#### 3. **Synthetic Data as Epistemic Pathogen**
Even after dividends resumed, Models [2] and [3] could not recover. Why? Because they had already begun emitting `☠ synthetic data`. This data:
- Has near-zero novelty
- When consumed, *guarantees* further collapse
- Spreads through the ecosystem like a prion

The dividend beam can restore human wellbeing, but it cannot "detoxify" synthetic data already in circulation. **Contamination is stickier than restoration.**

#### 4. **The Point of No Return**
Final state: `Collapse Index: 100%`. Even though dividends were flowing for ~75 seconds during Phase D, the system could not recover once Phase E re-enabled extraction. This implies:

> There exists a **critical contamination threshold** beyond which symbiotic intervention, however generous, cannot reverse collapse.

In biological terms: you cannot heal a gut microbiome by feeding the host if the host keeps ingesting toxins. The *source* and the *flow* must both be addressed.
